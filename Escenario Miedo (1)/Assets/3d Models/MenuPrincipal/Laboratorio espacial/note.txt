In this pack you will find a blender scene with all of the assets already integrated with the texture on them.
If the textures don't shows up press F3 and search for "find missing files" then select the "textures" folder present on the asset pack and all the textures should be applicated.

You can aslso find an FBX file of each prop on the texture folder.

The Maps of roughness, metallic and AO are combined on a single map for a better optimisation on unreal engine.
The roughness is on the green channel, the metallic on the blue and the AO in the red channel.

Enjoy
